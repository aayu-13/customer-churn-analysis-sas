# Customer Renewal & Churn Analysis (SAS)

## 📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer churn behavior using SAS to identify patterns and support customer retention strategies. The goal is to help businesses understand why customers leave and how to reduce churn.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall churn rate
* Identify factors influencing customer churn
* Segment high-risk customers
* Generate insights to support renewal strategies

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies

* SAS (PROC SQL, Data Step)
* SAS Viya
* Data Analysis & KPI Tracking

---

## 📁 Dataset

* Telco Customer Churn Dataset
* Contains customer demographics, services, billing, and churn status

---

## 🔍 Key Analysis Performed

### 1. Churn Rate Analysis

* Calculated overall churn percentage
* Found that ~26% of customers churned

### 2. Churn by Contract Type

* Month-to-month customers showed highest churn
* Long-term contracts (1–2 years) had significantly lower churn

### 3. High-Risk Customer Identification

* Identified customers with low tenure and high monthly charges
* Found that high-risk customers had significantly higher churn rate

---

## 📈 Key Insights

* Customers with short-term contracts are more likely to churn
* High monthly charges increase churn probability
* Early-stage customers (low tenure) are at higher risk

---

## 💡 Business Impact

* Helps identify customers at risk of leaving
* Supports customer retention and renewal strategies
* Enables data-driven decision-making

---

## 🚀 Conclusion

This project demonstrates how SAS can be used to analyze customer behavior and generate actionable insights to improve business outcomes.
